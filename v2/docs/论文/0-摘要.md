# 摘要

油中溶解气体分析是判断油浸式变压器内部状态的重要手段。设备出现异常时，可以依靠油中溶解气体的检测结果判断变压器内部的实际情况。但检测分级、产气趋势分析、故障类型判定以及报告编写这些实用方法，分别散落在不同的行业标准文件里，没有整合在一起。针对220kV及以下油浸式变压器，本文结合现有行业标准要求，把这几项方法落实到故障判定的具体步骤中，再通过智能体把整套分析流程固定下来，最终搭建出一套可以复核校验的在线分析流程和原型系统。

本文开展四项研究工作：一是依据DL/T 1498.2—2025表A.3开展检测分级，结合DL/T 722—2014相对产气速率计算总烃月环比，确定处置紧急度，对气体快速增长情况发出涨势预警。二是建立多规则协同故障判别机制，以注意值2、告警或相对产气速率超注意为触发条件，并行采用特征气体法、三比值法、大卫三角形法，完成性质核对、可信度标注，给出故障类型及试验建议。三是设计七步分析智能体工作流，依次完成气体输入、检测分级、产气趋势、处置紧急度、故障判型、监测决策、报告生成；判定依靠标准规则，大模型只润色报告文本，报告按DL/T 722—2014附录G组织，标准依据记入运行日志。四是开发原型系统，实现流程编排、监测决策、报告生成、PDF导出及分析助手，各页面共用同一套算法结果。

经标准案例验证，本文方法与DL/T 1685—2017附录D真实色谱样本的检测分级、处置紧急度评价结果完全吻合。基于DL/T 722—2014附录E的17组故障样本测试表明，本文方法对变压器故障大类性质的判别准确率可达88.2%。

关键词：油中溶解气体分析；智能体；在线监测；故障诊断；电力变压器

# Abstract

The analysis of dissolved gas in oil is an important means to judge the internal state of oil-immersed transformers. Therefore, when the equipment is abnormal, it can rely on the detection results of dissolved gas in oil to judge the actual situation inside the transformer. However, these practical methods, such as detection classification, gas production trend analysis, fault type determination and report writing, are scattered in different industry standard files and are not integrated together. For 220 kV and below oil-immersed transformers, this paper combines the existing industry standard requirements, implements these methods into the specific steps of fault determination, and then fixes the entire analysis process through the agent, and finally builds a set of online analysis process and prototype system that can be reviewed and verified.

This paper carried out four research work: First, according to DL/T 1498.2—2025 Table A.3 to carry out the detection classification, combined with DL/T 722—2014 relative gas production rate to calculate the total hydrocarbon month ring ratio, determine the disposal urgency, and issue a rising trend warning for the rapid growth of gas. The second is to establish a multi-rule collaborative fault discrimination mechanism, with the attention value 2, alarm or relative gas production rate over-attention as the trigger condition, and the characteristic gas method, three-ratio method, and Duval triangle method are used in parallel to complete the nature check and credibility labeling, and give fault types and test suggestions. The third is to design a seven-step analysis agent workflow to complete gas input, detection classification, gas production trend, disposal urgency, fault identification, monitoring decision-making, and report generation in turn. The judgment depends on the standard rules, and the large model only polishes the report text. The report is organized according to Appendix G of DL/T 722—2014, and the standard basis is recorded in the operation log. The fourth is to develop a prototype system to realize process scheduling, monitoring and decision-making, report generation, PDF export and analysis assistant, and each page shares the same set of algorithm results.

It was verified by standard cases that the discriminant results of this method were completely consistent with the detection classification of real chromatographic samples in Appendix D of DL/T 1685—2017 and the evaluation results of disposal urgency. The test results of 17 groups of fault samples based on DL/T 722—2014 Appendix E show that the accuracy of the proposed method for distinguishing the nature of transformer faults can reach 88.2%.

Keywords: analysis of dissolved gas in oil; intelligent agent; online monitoring; fault diagnosis; power transformer
